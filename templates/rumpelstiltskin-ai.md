# Rumpelstiltskin AI: photo and video guide

Replace the left dancer, right spectator or both characters. Each mode prepares its own scene and preset barn and footwork images for a new 15-second scene.

**[Open Rumpelstiltskin AI on ViralRemake](https://viralremake.com/templates/rumpelstiltskin-ai?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=viralremake_templates&utm_content=rumpelstiltskin-ai_create)**

## Choose the right photos

Add 2 separate photos in the role order shown. Keep every face clear; full-body photos work best.

- **Dancer or spectator:** one photo for the selected role.
- **Both:** two separate role photos.

For a human role, use an adult photo you have permission to transform. Keep each identity in its own input slot.

## What makes this format work

Decide whether you want to replace the dancer, spectator or both. The scene uses different positions for those roles, so label your photos before uploading. The reference preview is 13 seconds and generation is 15 seconds; those durations describe different assets.

## Reference and timing notes

- One photo for dancer or spectator; two photos for both
- 13-second reference preview; 15-second generation
- Two scene stills and a prepared six-shot scene
- Synchronized reference soundtrack included

A reference clip, selected generation cut and final output can have different durations. Confirm the model, cut and price shown in the live creator before purchasing. A reference is an example of the intended scene; it is not a guarantee of identical output.

## Audio and download

The ViralRemake template lists a reference soundtrack. Listen to the selected preview and check the live sound note. Reference voices are not a clone of your voice, and downloading does not transfer music ownership.

## Create and review

1. Open the template and watch the selected reference.
2. Choose a role mode where offered, then upload one suitable photo for each selected slot.
3. Check the available model, cut, sound note and checkout details before starting.
4. Review the completed MP4 for identity, hands or paws, movement and the scene-specific details described above. Keep the original download before editing.

AI likeness and movement can vary. Do not upload private payment details or personal photos to this public repository.
