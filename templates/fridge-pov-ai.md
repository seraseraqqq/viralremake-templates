## Choose the right photos

Use one clear photo of one cat or dog, with its face, ears, fur markings and front of the body visible. No group photos or covered faces. Only the foreground cake thief is replaced.

- **Your pet steals the cake:** 1 photo, assigned to pet.

For a human role, use an adult photo you have permission to transform. Keep each identity in its own input slot.

## What makes this format work

Only the foreground cake thief is replaced. The background watching dog is part of the scene. Choose one cat or dog photo showing its face, ears, fur markings and front body. Check that the same pet returns after the door-close blackout.
