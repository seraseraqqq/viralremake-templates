## Choose the right photos

One full-body adult photo, both feet visible. Your own clothes and shoes guide the scorer. Avoid groups and covered faces.

For a human role, use an adult photo you have permission to transform. Keep each identity in its own input slot.

## What makes this format work

Use a full-body adult photo with both feet visible. Clothes and shoes help define the goal scorer. This is a landscape goal sequence, so check the ball, feet and player continuity through the chip, pause and final finish.
