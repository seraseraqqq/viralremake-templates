## Choose the right photos

Add a photo of the center figure. Optionally add a second photo to replace the background choir.

- **The center figure:** One consenting adult, face and whole body visible.
- **Background choir (optional):** Optional · replaces the choir members.

For a human role, use an adult photo you have permission to transform. Keep each identity in its own input slot.

## What makes this format work

The still center figure and moving choir create the contrast. Assign the center photo first. If you use the optional choir role, upload its photo separately and check the selected casting before paying.

## Reference and timing notes

- 1 or 2 photos: center figure, optional choir members
- 13.8-second selected clip

- Synchronized reference soundtrack included

A reference clip, selected generation cut and final output can have different durations. Confirm the model, cut and price shown in the live creator before purchasing. A reference is an example of the intended scene; it is not a guarantee of identical output.
