## Choose the right photos

Choose a clear photo of one person or pet per selected role. Keep the face, ears and markings visible. Single-role modes need one photo; both-character mode needs two.

- **Replace the lead rapper:** 1 photo, assigned to lead.
- **Replace the speaker holder:** 1 photo, assigned to holder.
- **Replace both performers:** 2 photos, assigned to lead, holder.

For a human role, use an adult photo you have permission to transform. Keep each identity in its own input slot.

## What makes this format work

Choose the rapper, the speaker holder, or both before preparing photos. The lead and holder are different roles; assigning the same group image to both makes identity harder to follow. For a two-person remake, use two separate photos.
